Two choices made while building them:
- Infighting only applies to regular enemies' shots. Boss patterns never hit their escorts, and
  bosses can't be hit by stray fire, so every boss fight stays as designed.

  The worst case (64 bolts against 42 enemies) costs about 0.14 ms a frame, measured in isolation.

## 1. What the research says

### 1.1 Mid-90s DOS action and flight shooters

- **Difficulty scaled several levers at once, not just enemy health.**
  - Descent had Trainee, Rookie, Hotshot, Ace and Insane. Higher levels changed:
    - robot fire rate, accuracy and dodging;
    - starting missiles (7 down to 3);
    - shield and energy pickup value (18 down to 6);
    - the reactor escape timer (50 s down to 30 s);
    - how many robots the generators produce.

    Sources: https://www.gamers.org/games/descent/intro.html ·
    https://descent-descent-ii-descent-3-video-game-wiki.fandom.com/wiki/Descent_by_Star_Man_Part_1:_Game_Information *(summary)*
  - Doom's five skills: the easiest halves the damage you take and doubles ammo. Nightmare
    makes monsters faster and brings them back to life. https://doom.fandom.com/wiki/Skill_level
  - Heretic's easiest level uses emergency healing for you automatically, an early "assist".
    https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/562682-heretic/faqs/36537 *(summary)*
  - Tyrian's harder levels raised enemy health and fire rate.
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrian_(video_game)
  - **For us:** presets change fire cadence, shot speed, damage taken, pickup value and how long
    enemies telegraph an attack. They never change enemy count, HP, layout or level seed.
- **Saving.**
  - Descent saved between levels only; its designers thought saving anywhere made it too easy.
    Your weapons dropped where you died.
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descent_(video_game)
  - Doom let you save anywhere, and every level is winnable from the starting kit ("pistol
    start"). https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Pistol_start
  - **For us:** a checkpoint at sector start plus one at each cleared arena, and the starting kit
    stays viable in every sector.
- **Onboarding** was a manual, a briefing, a demo loop playing on the title screen
  (https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Demo), and a first level that teaches without text. 3.0's attract
  flythrough already covers the demo loop.
- **Feel.**
  - Doom's monster infighting: a monster hit by another's stray shot turns on it, and only
    ranged or splash attackers start fights. https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Monster_infighting
  - Doom's whole-screen palette flashes for damage and pickups.
    https://doomwiki.org/wiki/PLAYPAL
  - Afterburners: Descent II's is mostly felt through sound and its HUD gauge.
    https://tcrf.net/Proto:Descent_II_(PC) *(summary)*

### 1.2 Web and mobile flying games today

- **Time to first fun.**
  - Poki: under 10 s, and the first session matters more than in app stores.
    https://developers.poki.com/guide/easy-access *(summary)*
  - CrazyGames: under 20 s to gameplay; tutorials worded for the device ("tap" vs "click");
    touch controls must be on screen.
    https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/gameplay/ *(summary)*
  - Portals also expect the game to pause when the tab loses focus.
- **Accessibility.**
  - Offer a wide range of difficulty, and don't give the easy level a belittling name. That
    rules out joke names like "too young to die" under our child-friendly rule.
    https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/offer-a-wide-choice-of-difficulty-levels/
  - Let players change difficulty mid-game without losing progress.
    https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/allow-difficulty-level-to-be-altered-during-gameplay-either-through-settings-or-adaptive-difficulty/
  - Celeste's Assist Mode, with separate toggles, is the reference model.
    https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/celeste-assist-mode/
  - Camera shake and FOV kicks must be optional (Xbox Accessibility Guideline 117).
    https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/117
  - No more than 3 flashes per second.
    https://w3c.github.io/wcag21/understanding/three-flashes-or-below-threshold.html
- **Saving in the browser.**
  - Godot's `user://` on the web is an in-memory filesystem copied to IndexedDB asynchronously.
    A save made just before a tab closes, or while the page is hidden, can be lost.
    https://github.com/godotengine/godot/issues/39643
  - One project worked around this by also writing a synchronous `localStorage` copy.
    https://github.com/RichardAtCT/greening-terra/pull/9
  - Safari deletes a site's script-written storage after 7 days of Safari use without a visit.
    Home-screen apps keep their own counter.
    https://webkit.org/blog/14403/updates-to-storage-policy/
  - A `navigator.storage.persist()` request may exempt the site from eviction; Safari decides
    silently.
  - **For us:** mirror each checkpoint synchronously to `localStorage` when it is written, and
    load whichever copy is newer.
- **iOS.**
  - Web Audio unlocks only on the end of a tap. Setting `navigator.audioSession.type =
    "playback"` (Safari 16.4+) stops the hardware mute switch silencing it.
    https://github.com/feross/unmute-ios-audio
  - Godot 4 web builds hit "WebGL context lost" crashes under iOS 18 memory pressure, so the
    page should offer a reload when it happens.
    https://forum.godotengine.org/t/webgl-context-loss-and-app-crash-in-godot-4-3-exported-web-on-ios-browsers/81024
  - On iPhone, adding to the Home Screen is the only reliable way to get fullscreen.
- **Launch.**
  - Play button above the fold.
  - A 1200×630 Open Graph / `summary_large_image` link-preview image.
    https://og-image.org/learn/twitter-card-size
  - A capture video that hooks the viewer in the first 3 s.
    https://presskit.gg/field-guides/game-marketing-gifs-guide
  - Post to one subreddit at a time, disclose that you're the developer, and read each
    subreddit's current rules first.

### New gaps 3.0 introduced (not in any plan)

- **The Gemini icon breaks rule 1.** `assets/branding/app_icon_1024.png` (1 MB, generated in
  Gemini, commit `c9488b4`) ships as the favicon. It contradicts hard rule 1 and the README's "no
  imported assets" line.
- **The D11 install nudge can never appear.** `export_presets.cfg` has
  `progressive_web_app/enabled=false` and no manifest, so `beforeinstallprompt` never fires and
  the buttons at `overlays.gd:778-799` never show.
- **Touch has no pause button.** `pause_game` is bound only to Enter, Esc and Start
  (`input_setup.gd:29-30,61`).
- **Nothing pauses the game when the tab loses focus.** No notification handling in `game.gd`.
- **Two blinks ignore REDUCE FLASH:** the THREAT lamp (≈2.8 Hz) and the low-shield LED
  (2.5 Hz) (`hud.gd:283,297`). Both are small areas.
- **`SpriteForge` bake cost is unmeasured on the web.** Nothing records the bake time or whether
  it used the GPU or silently fell back. `tests/forge_probe.gd` prints a time, but no figure has
  been logged.
- **One gauntlet frame-time outlier.** A single 8–10 ms step in 1 of 8 headless runs
  (`tests/perf_baseline.md`). Watch for it on iPad.

## 3. Findings by plan

### 3.1 M5: difficulty, checkpoints, onboarding

**Where the difficulty levers live today.** The plan assumed one place for each; 3.0 spread them
out.

- **Regular enemies bake speed and fire interval when they spawn** (`enemy_manager.gd:211-212`).
  Arena enemies all spawn when the level loads (`game.gd:392-406`). A multiplier applied at spawn
  therefore can't respond to a mid-run change, so apply it where the value is used.
- **Shot speeds are hard-coded numbers at each firing site:**
  - drone 26 (`em:338`), turret 24 (`em:292`);
  - boss aimed shot 32 (`em:579`), boss volley 30 (`em:653`);
  - ring bursts (`_ring_burst`, `em:497`, used by the SPINNER and the Rift Maw) and the
    Maw spiral (`_spiral_shot`, `em:509`).
  - **All of them go through `shot_mgr.fire_enemy`** (`shot_manager.gd:230`), which is the single
    place to scale them.
- **Boss pattern timers are hard-coded numbers:**
  - Sentinel volleys every 3.2/2.2 s and summons every 6 s (`em:589,594`);
  - Brood Mother summons every 7/5.5/4 s and lays mines every 4.5/3.5 s (`em:605,610`);
  - Rift Maw rings every 3.6/4.4 s, plus a spiral burst every 0.11 s (`em:628-640`).
- **STINGER and MINE timings:**
  - STINGER dive speed 52 and wind-up 0.65 s (`em:40-42`);
  - MINE arming radius 14 and fuse 0.6 s (`em:45-46`).
- **Seeker threshold.** Turrets switch to seeking shots when `level.enemy_speed >= 9`
  (`em:221`). Scaling `level.enemy_speed` directly would change which turrets fire seekers.
- **Every source of player damage already goes through `player.take_damage`** (`player.gd:329`):
  shots, collisions, the STINGER, mines, crushers and walls. That makes it the one place to apply
  damage taken.
- **Hit-stop and the automap both reset `Engine.time_scale` to 1.0** (`gib_manager.gd:74`,
  `automap_display.gd:95`). A game-speed assist has to become the base those two restore to.

**Checkpoints.**

- **What persists today.** No mid-level save exists. A tab close keeps only `unlocked_level`.
  Score, weapon marks, plasma bombs and the unbanked salvage are lost
  (`game_state.gd:291-316`).
- **Fields the plan's snapshot left out:**
  - `weapon_marks` and `salvage_run`
  - power-up timers
  - level stats and `elapsed`, which feed the rank
  - cleared arenas and door states
- **Placing the ship.** `snap_ring` (`player.gd:352`) only sets `ring_idx`, and
  `reset_to_start()` always puts the ship at ring 1. Resuming needs a real `place_at_ring` that
  faces the ship along `ring.d`.
- **The briefing can't be skipped on resume.** It builds the world and warms the web shaders
  (`game.gd:796-898`), so a resume must still go through it.

**Pause and settings.**

- **The pause panel has no buttons, and a click anywhere resumes** (`overlays.gd:334-341`,
  `game.gd:1197`). There is no way back to the title after launching.
- **Settings can only return to the start screen.** `_settings_return` has no pause route.
- **The settings screen is full**, with 4 steppers and 8 toggles, so difficulty needs its own
  panel.

**Onboarding.**

- **What a first-time player meets:**
  1. The photosensitivity notice.
  2. The title over the attract flythrough.
  3. The briefing, whose LAUNCH button already works as a skip.
  4. Flight, with no prompts.
- **Gaps:**
  - The FLIGHT MANUAL covers keyboard, mouse and pad only, not touch.
  - L1 is the only sector whose briefing has no tip.
  - Tunnel enemies spawn at random, one roll per ring (`game.gd:1169-1177`), so "first enemy
    within ~15 rings" needs code, not a `.tres` edit.
  - L1 already has no crushers (`game.gd:464`).
- **The skip-button task (old Task 6) is already done:** LAUNCH skips the briefing, and there is
  no auto-advance timer.

### 3.2 M6: feel

- **Infighting:**
  - **Damage scale.** The plan's bolt damage is `SHOT_DMG` (9, a player-shields number), but
    enemy HP is small integers. A NEUTRON hit does 1. Every stray bolt would kill outright, so
    use 1 HP per bolt.
  - **Shooter tracking.** It tracks the shooter by array index, which shifts on every
    `remove_at`. Carry the firing node instead.
  - **Hit radius.** It uses a shared `HIT_R2`, but each enemy now carries its own `e.hit_r2`
    (`em:213,242`).
  - **Spawn overlap.** Shots spawn inside the shooter's own hit radius, so skip the shooter and
    each shot's first 0.15 s.
  - **Scoring.** Its "infighting never scores" rule contradicts current behaviour: mine chains
    and fuel-cell blasts already score through `_hurt` (`em:736-746`). Infighting kills should
    score too.
- **Boost:** there is no FOV change and no one-shot sound today. Only continuous engine pitch
  (`audio_sys.gd:107`). Gate the FOV pulse on SCREEN SHAKE, which already gates the kick
  (`player.gd:358`), rather than on REDUCE ROLL. On touch, boost is a double-tap toggle
  (`touch_controls.gd:245-253`), and detecting when boost starts still works there.
- **Stale evidence in the plan:**
  - The live hit flash is `SpriteForge._flash_of` (`sprite_forge.gd:171`), not `sprite_gen`.
  - `GIB_TINTS` lives in `enemy_manager.gd:87`, not `gib_manager.gd`.

### 3.3 M7: baked sprites (retired)

- **3.0 already covers it.** `SpriteModels` builds all 7 enemies (hulk and turret included),
  3 distinct bosses and 8 pickups from primitives. `SpriteForge.bake()` turntable-renders them at
  boot.
- **The player's presence is the first-person cockpit** (`hud.gd:121-132`, `HudArt.console()`).
  There's no ship to bake.
- **No rule change is needed.** The M0.1 change to hard rule 1 is withdrawn; with the icon
  replaced (Step 1) the rule holds literally again.
- **What's left is measurement:**
  - bake time, and whether it used the GPU, on iPad and a mid-range phone;
  - sprite shimmer at fog distance. The sprites use nearest filtering with no mipmaps, and cells
    are 64/128 px.

### 3.4 M8: landing page, `/play`, capture

- **Export wiring:** `build.sh:38` → `dist/index.html`. The shell's `$GODOT_*` placeholders are
  relative, so a subdirectory export works.
- **A bare `/play` without the trailing slash** would resolve `index.js` against the site root.
  Add a redirect from `/play` to `/play/`.
- **Other things the move touches:**
  - The favicon moves with the game, so the landing page needs its own.
  - `README.md:7` and its local-build commands, and `serve_dev.py`, have root URLs.
  - GoatCounter is unaffected, because events use explicit names.
- **The design baseline is gone.** The start screen is now a chrome logo and DOS windows over
  attract mode (`overlays.gd:231-271`, colours `:23-34`). The landing page should use that
  language: DOS window chrome in CSS plus the Step 1 code-made logo, with no imported art.
- **PWA and scope.** A service worker registered at the site root would keep serving the cached
  old game at `/` after the landing page takes over. So app-install mode ships in the same change
  as the `/play/` move, never before it.
  - Godot's service worker is cache-first, so a new deploy reaches a returning player one visit
    late unless the page activates a waiting worker.
  - iOS has no `beforeinstallprompt`. It needs an "Add to Home Screen" hint instead.
- **Capture:**
  - Movie Maker needs a real renderer (`xvfb-run`, not `--headless`).
  - It records audio, which needs stripping.
  - Window captures include the CRT filter, which is on by default.
  - `tests/screenshot_probe.gd:68-167` already stages combat and the boss, so it's a better base
    than `capture_verify.gd`.

### 3.5 M9: hygiene, gates, posting

- **Already exists:**
  - the build stamp (`build_info.gd:13`, shown on the start and pause screens);
  - README sections for 3.0 features, controls, power-ups, testing, beta feedback and privacy;
  - the MIT `LICENSE`.
- **Missing:** `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/` and the D7 "players, not contributors" sentence.
- **README drift:**
  - no mention of touch or phone support;
  - says "Godot 4.x" though the build pins 4.7;
  - the "no imported assets" claim, until Step 1 lands;
  - the "dies into black" intro, which contradicts 3.0's vivid look;
  - `forge_probe` isn't listed.
- **Gates already covered by 3.0 phase 7:**
  - smoke test;
  - rendered probe and a gallery of all nine levels;
  - GLES3 driver pass;
  - perf probe;
  - a soak run where every boss reaches every phase;
  - web boot in headless Chromium.
- **Still open:**
  - a human full campaign;
  - real Chrome, Firefox, Safari, iOS and Android;
  - gamepad;
  - John's screenshot verdict.
- **Posting pitch for r/godot:** now "Godot turntable-bakes its own sprites at boot, and a GPU
  lookup table quantizes the frame to 256 colours". The Blender angle is gone.

## 4. Revised sequence

One PR at a time from the working branch, with a playtest on the Netlify deploy preview before
each gameplay change merges. Every step passes the gates in §5.

**Step 0: this document.** Old plans get a "revised" banner; M7 is marked retired.

**Step 1: code-made app icon.**
- `tools/make_icons.gd` paints the chrome "VR" mark from `LogoGen`/`PixelFont`/`Palette` on the
  CPU, so it works headless. It outputs the favicon, PWA icons (144/180/512) and a 1200×630
  link-preview image.
- The output is generated by `build.sh` before export and not committed.
- The Gemini PNG is deleted.

**Step 2: M5a, pause menu and focus.**
- The pause menu becomes a DOS window: RESUME / DIFFICULTY (once Step 3 lands) / SETTINGS /
  FLIGHT MANUAL / QUIT TO TITLE.
- A pause button on touch.
- Auto-pause when the window or tab loses focus.
- QUIT TO TITLE rebuilds the attract world.

**Step 3: M5b, difficulty presets and assist.**
- Values are read at the moment they're used, so pause-menu changes apply at once:
  - damage taken → `player.take_damage`;
  - shot speed → `shot_mgr.fire_enemy`;
  - fire cadence → the refire resets and boss timers;
  - warning time → STINGER wind-up and MINE fuse;
  - pickup value → `pickup_manager`.
- Never enemy count, HP, layout, seed or `level.enemy_speed`.
- Game-speed assist becomes a base time scale that hit-stop and the automap restore to.
- Starting numbers (RECRUIT / RUNNER / VOIDBORNE), to tune in playtest:

  | Value | RECRUIT | RUNNER | VOIDBORNE |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Fire interval | ×1.35 | ×1.0 | ×0.8 |
  | Shot speed | ×0.8 | ×1.0 | ×1.15 |
  | Damage taken | ×0.6 | ×1.0 | ×1.3 |
  | Warning time | ×1.4 | ×1.0 | ×0.8 |
  | Pickup value | ×1.5 | ×1.0 | ×0.75 |

- A DIFFICULTY panel opens from the main menu, the pause menu and game over.
- After a second death in the same sector, game over suggests RECRUIT.

**Step 4: M5c, checkpoints.**
- **When saved:** at sector start, and at every bulkhead opening on RECRUIT and RUNNER.
  VOIDBORNE saves at sector start only, era-style.
- **Where stored:** `user://checkpoint.cfg` plus a synchronous `localStorage` copy; the newer one
  wins. A corrupt file is ignored.
- **Resuming:** CONTINUE → briefing → RESUME. Cleared arenas stay open and the ship is placed at
  the checkpoint ring.
- **On death:** game over offers RETRY FROM CHECKPOINT or RESTART SECTOR.

**Step 5: M5d, the first 90 seconds.**
- **A deliberate departure from PLAN.md's "without adding text":** first-run cockpit callouts
  through `hud.show_message`, worded for touch, keyboard or gamepad, one at a time, each
  retired once you've done it: STEER → FIRE → BOOST → EVADE → BULKHEAD.
- A guaranteed first drone near ring 12 in sector 1.
- An L1 briefing tip, and a touch page in the FLIGHT MANUAL.
- `audioSession.type = "playback"` for iOS.
- Measure the time from page load to the first controllable frame.

**Step 6: M6, feel (corrected).**
- Infighting as described in §3.2. Stretch goal: a short "grudge" where the victim fires back.
- A boost FOV pulse (+6°, gated by SCREEN SHAKE) and a one-shot swell sound.
- The THREAT and low-shield blinks stay lit under REDUCE FLASH.

**Then v4, the content pass** (`docs/revamp/2026-09-30-v4-content-plan.md`): palette tricks,
the enemy roster, hubs with keys and objectives, and the intermission and episode rituals. It lands
before the public launch in Step 8, and its bake-time item depends on Step 7's measurements.

**Step 7: M7′, real-device verification.**
- Report bake time and GPU status through the boot diagnostics.
- A "tap to reload" screen when the WebGL context is lost.
- A fog-distance shimmer check. Add a lower-resolution set for distant sprites only if needed.
- A device pass by John.

**Step 8: M8, landing page, `/play` and PWA together.**
- Landing page in the 3.0 language, with PLAY above the fold, a small code-drawn canvas tunnel
  and the link-preview image.
- App-install mode ships in the same change, with service-worker update handling and an iOS
  "Add to Home Screen" hint.
- Measure compressed transfer size.
- A capture video recorded under xvfb, for Reddit only and not committed.

**Step 9: M9, hygiene, human gates and staged posting,** as in §3.5.

**Not doing:** sector passwords, hidden auto-difficulty, the Blender pipeline or a rule-1
exception, and scaling enemy HP or count.

## 5. Gates for every step

1. `godot --headless --import`, then `godot --headless tests/smoke_test.tscn` with zero

2. The perf probe on L8, the L9 boss and the gauntlet, with no new step over 8 ms.
3. A rendered probe under `xvfb-run` for every new or changed screen.
4. A Web export booting in headless Chromium.
5. For gameplay steps, John's playtest on the deploy preview before merge.
