VOIDLINE privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

The short version

VOIDLINE has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, your email address, your location, your contacts, your camera or your microphone.

The iPhone and iPad app

The app collects no data at all. Your settings, scores and saved progress stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app. The app does not connect to the internet while you play.

If you choose FEEDBACK in the menu, the app opens a short form in your web browser. The form is optional. Whatever you type there is sent to us through Tally, and we use it only to improve the game. Please don't put personal details in it.

The web version

The web version counts four anonymous things: that the game loaded, that a sector was started, that a sector was finished, and that the feedback form was opened. The sector number is the whole message. There are no cookies and nothing that could single anyone out. The counting is done by GoatCounter, which is open source. Settings and saved progress stay in your own browser.

Children

The game is suitable for children and collects nothing from them, or from anyone.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions are welcome through the feedback form in the game.
